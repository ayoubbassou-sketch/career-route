          Projects and keywords

          An item joins a project when its title, text, meeting, sender or channel contains one of the keywords. Accents and capitals are ignored. Separate keywords with commas.

          How it works

          Sources. Google Calendar, the next two weeks (meetings you declined are hidden). Circleback action items assigned to you (pending, last 30 days). Gmail inbox from the last 7 days, unread or starred, skipping threads where you sent the last reply, without promotions, social, updates and no-reply senders. Slack DMs and @mentions from the last 4 days, bots excluded, skipping anything you already answered in that DM or thread. Due dates are read from words like “demain”, “vendredi”, “sous une semaine” or a written date; set your own on any item.

          Claude triage. When new items arrive, Claude reads them: what kind they are (action, reply expected, FYI, automatic), urgency, importance, due date, project and a clear title for the need. It links items that ask for the same thing through different channels into one task, and learns from the moves and rules you made. Your own choices always win. Turn it off from the status line.

          Sorting without Claude. Each item gets an urgency score (age, deadline words, DMs, questions, fresh client mail) and an importance score (meeting commitments, client projects, external senders, production or blockers). Open an item to see why it landed where it did, or move it yourself. In Decide now, press T1 on anything quick: it jumps to the top of Do now. Your choice always wins, then Claude's, then the score.

          Planning. Open an item and pick a free slot, or press “Pick on calendar” and click a time in My planning. It waits in Schedule and becomes T1 when the slot starts. With “Also block it in Google Calendar”, a private event is created in your calendar. Unplanning does not delete that event.

          What changes outside this page. Planning with the calendar option creates a private Google Calendar event. Ticking a Circleback item marks it done in Circleback, and Undo reopens it. Gmail and Slack are only read.
